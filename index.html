How To Play

Guess the hidden word! You get 6 attempts for 5-6 letter words, and 7 attempts for 7-8 letter words.

A
Correct Spot: The letter is in the word and in the right position.
B
Wrong Spot: The letter is in the word, but in a different position.
C
Not Included: The letter is not part of the word.
