How to Play

Find the hidden word in 6 guesses (7 for 7- and 8-letter words). Each guess must be a real word. After you press Enter, the tiles change color to show how close you got.

More to Explore
  • Options: pick 5–8 letter words, choose how familiar they are, and set music and sound.
  • Theme Studio: try a preset or mix your own colors, then share your theme code.
  • Challenge friends: after a game, tap "Share Result Grid" to copy your emoji board with a link to the same word.
  • Statistics: wins, streaks and guess counts are tracked for each word length.
  • Install it: add Loxie to your home screen to play like an app, even offline. On Android use the download icon in the header; on iPhone tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
